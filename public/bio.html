About

Riley Johnson

Owner and lead inspector.

After receiving my business administration degree in 2011 from CSU Chico, immediately took on an administrative and marketing position with a prominent local real estate brokerage. Within a few months I took on the added responsibility of processing the few short sales and foreclosures coming through our office. 1 year later, our office was processing more distressed properties than any other brokerage in Redding.

After spending some time in both the paperwork and rehabilitation side of the industry, I decided my skills were best utilized helping prospective buyers understand the true condition of homes they wish to purchase. 12 years later, there's nothing else I would rather be doing. I have a passion for home inspections, and it is not a side gig; I inspect homes every day of the week, often 10 to 12 hours per day. I look forward to the opportunity of serving you and your family during what is likely the most consequential financial decision of your life.

What you get

Our base fee of $395 is competitive with the few other local CREIA Certified home inspectors. We offer free reinspections and do not charge extra for a home's age, or whether it's on a raised foundation. As usual, reports are delivered same-day via email using 3D Inspection Software, the most dynamic and professional platform. We accept cash, checks, and credit cards both online and over the phone.

With over 7000 inspections performed over the past 11 years, no single home inspector in California performs more inspections. You can be sure that our reports are written to the highest standard of detail and professionalism, and I am always available to answer buyer's questions after the inspection. We look forward to another year of being the most trusted choice in Northern California. Thanks for your continuing business this year!
